Rows with errors stay out of the prepared export. Blank PFAS concentrations remain explicitly unavailable and are never converted to zero. Connected water bodies are calculated as upstream rivers + downstream rivers + directly connected lakes/reservoirs.

German public sources: WISE Freshwater (micropollution), Eurostat API (population density), Copernicus EU-Hydro (network connectivity), and an official PFAS measurement dataset where available.

Missing PFAS values are labelled unavailable. Do not upload personal or household-level data. A production deployment requires authentication, authorization, encryption, audit logging, retention controls, and tenant separation before storing operator data.
